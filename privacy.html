Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Data controller: [Yayıncı adı], [Yayıncı adresi]

  • Kelime Yıldızı asks only for a nickname, a colour and the school grade for your child.
  • Learning progress, play time and settings are stored on this device only and are not sent to us. The app also keeps a random install ID on this device.
  • If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, this information can also be in your backup. We can't access your backups.
  • The parent PIN is stored securely on this device.
  • Packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • If you have turned on sharing crash reports with Apple in Settings, those reports may reach us anonymously through Apple.
  • To delete everything: First use Parent area → "Tüm verileri sil" (Delete all data), then delete the app from the device. Deleting only the app does not remove the parent PIN record from the device. You can delete old device backups in Apple's settings or on your computer.
  • For questions and requests under Turkish data protection law (KVKK): [destek e-posta adresi]
